Synthetic audio fixtures for Phase 3.

clean_*  : macOS `say` output, 22050 Hz mono LEI16.
noisy_*  : the same file with Gaussian hiss + 50 Hz-ish hum mixed in at the stated SNR.
           snr15 = a quiet room, snr05 = a busy corridor, snr00 = signal and noise equal.

These exercise the DEGRADATION POLICY, not ASR accuracy. Measuring recognition accuracy
needs a real acoustic model and real recordings of real speakers; synthetic TTS in
synthetic noise would produce a number that means nothing. What these fixtures prove is
that low-confidence input degrades to touch instead of being recorded as a guess.
